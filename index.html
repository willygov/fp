Is FOMO available in my country?

Availability and rules differ by country. Check FOMO's terms and your local regulations before you sign up.

What does self-custody mean?

You control the wallet keys instead of a company holding your funds. If you lose your keys, nobody can restore access, so back them up offline.

What is a memecoin?

A token driven mostly by community and hype rather than a product. Prices can swing hard, so treat them as high risk.
